> Canonical language: English

## 1. Apache-2.0 patent grant

Morphz material identified as Apache-2.0 is subject to the express patent grant in Section 3 of the
[Apache License, Version 2.0](LICENSE). Each Contributor grants only those patent claims that the
Contributor can license and that are necessarily infringed by that Contributor's Contribution,
alone or in combination with the Work to which the Contribution was submitted.

The patent license terminates for a recipient who institutes patent litigation alleging that the
Work or an incorporated Contribution constitutes direct or contributory patent infringement, as
specified by Apache-2.0 Section 3.

## 2. No broader patent commitment

This policy does not grant rights to an entire patent portfolio, to excluded material listed in
[LICENSE_SCOPE.md](LICENSE_SCOPE.md), or to technology that is not covered by the Apache-2.0 patent
grant. Publication of a Draft specification does not create an additional standards-essential
patent covenant, FRAND commitment, non-assertion promise, or license for an independent
implementation beyond rights actually granted by Apache-2.0.

Before a Morphz specification advances to a status that invites independent production
implementations or formal compatibility claims, the project will determine whether a separate
standards-essential patent commitment is required and will subject any such commitment to explicit
adoption and legal review.

## 3. Contributions

Contributions intentionally submitted for inclusion in Apache-2.0 material are subject to Sections
3 and 5 of Apache-2.0 unless the copyright owner conspicuously marks the submission as
`Not a Contribution` or a separate written agreement applies. Contributors must have authority to
make the submission and the grants that accompany it. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## 4. Disclosures

A contributor who knows that a submitted Contribution may be covered by a patent application or
patent controlled by that contributor should disclose the application or publication number, the
potentially relevant claims, and the relationship to the Contribution in the pull request or to the
Project Lead before incorporation. A disclosure records information; it does not by itself expand
or restrict Apache-2.0.

## 5. Other rights

Apache-2.0 Section 3 and this policy do not grant trademark, certification, trade-secret, data, or
service-access rights. Trademark and compatibility claims are governed by
[TRADEMARKS.md](TRADEMARKS.md).

If this explanatory policy conflicts with Apache-2.0 for Apache-2.0 material, the Apache License
controls.
